# Crave Portal Demo: Video Walkthrough Script (about 90 seconds)

**Open:** `index.html` in Chrome, full screen (F11), browser zoom 100%, window 1440×900 or larger.
**Shortcut:** open `index.html#skip` to jump straight past the login.
**Hands-free option:** after logging in, click **Guided tour** (top bar). It walks through 11 steps automatically, about 6.5 s each. Use → / ← to step manually, and Esc to exit.

---

| # | Do this | Say this (voice-over) |
|---|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Login screen. Let the product slideshow play for about 3 s. | "This is the Environpac customer portal. Every customer gets their own secure login." |
| 2 | Click **Sign in securely**. | "When Crave signs in, everything is built around their brand." |
| 3 | Dashboard loads. Pause on the hero. | "Crave sees their logo, both locations, and exactly how much of their branded packaging we're holding for them." |
| 4 | Point at the 4 tiles. | "Stock on hand, what's on the way, what they've invested this year, and what's due. One view, at a glance." |
| 5 | Point at **Smart insights**. | "The portal watches their usage and warns early. Soup cups are down to about 2.5 weeks, so it suggests a reorder before they run out." |
| 6 | Scroll to the product cards and hover a couple. | "These are their actual products: bowls, soup cups, napkins, both pizza boxes and the bag. Each shows live stock, weeks of cover, and incoming stock." |
| 7 | Click the **16 oz Soup Cup** card → drawer opens. | "Click any product for specs, a 12-week stock trend and full order history." |
| 8 | Click **Reorder** → pick 10,000 → **Send request**. | "Reordering takes one click. The artwork is already on file. They see the cost, the deposit and the arrival date upfront." |
| 9 | Scroll to **Orders** + **Spend & payments**. | "Every order is tracked from artwork to warehouse, and every dollar is transparent: deposits, balances, spend by product." |
| 10 | Point at the ship card. | "Coming in Phase 2: live ocean tracking, so they can watch their container cross the ocean." |
| 10b | Click the gold **chat bubble** (bottom right) → tap *Where is my order?* → tap *Book a call*, pick a day and time → **Confirm booking**. | "And whenever they need us, they can chat with their account manager or book a call right from the portal. The invite goes straight into their calendar." |
| 11 | Toggle **Environpac admin** (top bar). | "Behind the scenes, our team updates stock, payments and orders in seconds…" |
| 12 | In Quick updates, set Stock to *16 oz Soup Cup*, *Received*, 5,000 → **Update stock**. Then switch back to **Customer view**. | "…and the customer's portal updates instantly. The soup cup alert clears on its own." |
| 13 | Back in admin, flip a **Visibility** toggle. | "We control exactly what each customer sees." |
| 14 | End on the Crave dashboard. | "Your packaging, at a glance. Built for Crave, and ready for every customer we serve." |

---

### Notes
- All numbers are sample data. Real quantities and payments get entered from the admin console.
- The demo resets on page refresh.
- Works offline (Chart.js is bundled). Fonts load from Google Fonts when online.
